Three findings that change what I'd build

40posts

Semi-private charging is a permission problem first

Owners describe months of negotiation with their housing society, sockets removed, and fear over fire risk and metering. The society committee decides, and the builder controls the wiring.

my society is not allowing me to install charging socketForum post, Jan 2021

Implication: society onboarding is a product of its own. Give the committee a one-page load and safety pack for its general meeting.

49posts

Trust breaks at the session start and at the money

Owners report holding the connector until it locks and charger screens stuck on "initialising". 33 of the 37 wallet posts came after the 2026 prepaid change.

we need to hold the connector until its locked at the gridsForum post, Feb 2025

Implication: AtherNode's society billing needs one per-session receipt that both the rider and the society can check.

302upvotes

At home, owners want control more than speed

The most-upvoted post in the set asks for a custom finish time for reaching 100%, instead of the fixed 5 am.

5 am is rubbish.Forum post, May 2024

Implication: in a building with a load cap, the promise to riders is "charged by the time I leave". That's the north-star metric.

Why load management is the core economics

If a society sizes its supply for every charger at full power, it pays for load it rarely uses. If it sizes for the energy riders actually need overnight, the requirement is far smaller. AtherNode distributes power so the building stays within its sanctioned load.

less load to sanction when charging is scheduled across the night
